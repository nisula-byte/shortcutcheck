Generated by `shortcutcheck mrs realism`. Git commit `dc693e3720b794cda5f492704268b1aac86fe4d1`. Do not edit by hand.

Distance: weighted Gower distance to the nearest real training app (0/1 flags compared by mismatch, numeric features by training percentile). Metadata and behaviour each carry half the weight, shared equally within each block, and constant columns carry none (decision D13). Threshold: the 95th percentile of distances from a training app to its nearest *other* training app (2,000-app seeded sample). Perturbed apps beyond the threshold are excluded from violation counts. Evaluation inputs: a seeded, class-stratified sample of up to 5,000 validation apps. Violation rates are not shown here, so the filter is fixed before any violation rate is seen.
