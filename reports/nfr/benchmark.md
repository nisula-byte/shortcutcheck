Target (NFR7): core relations (MR1, MR3, MR4 with the realism check) for one fitted model on 10,000 apps in at most 60 s and 2048 MB peak memory. Time covers fitting the relations and the realism check and running the gate (not training the model or loading data). Peak memory is the whole process, including data loading and model training.
